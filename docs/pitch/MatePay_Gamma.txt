MatePay

USDC checkout for Argentina

Price in pesos. Request payment in USDC.

QR payments and readable aliases, with network fees sponsored by MatePay as the next development milestone.

Hackathon prototype. Solana Devnet only.

---

The checkout problem

Our working hypothesis: small merchants and customers who already use USDC need a simpler checkout experience.

- Merchants enter prices in Argentine pesos.
- Customers need to understand the USDC amount and recognize the recipient.
- Holding SOL just to pay a network fee adds another step.

Merchant interviews and usability tests are still pending.

---

The MatePay experience

A merchant enters an ARS amount and generates a QR request with a USDC equivalent.

The customer reviews the recipient and amount before approving payment.

Readable aliases help identify the destination. The planned fee payer covers network fees in SOL.

Current stage: an interactive checkout simulation.

---

Merchant checkout

The prototype includes a numeric keypad, currency conversion and QR generation.

Illustrative example:

ARS 4,500 / ARS 1,240 per USDC = 3.63 USDC after rounding.

The rate service requests DolarAPI data and uses fallback values if requests fail.

This example is an estimate, not an executable exchange quote. The QR integration still needs wallet validation.

---

Customer checkout

The current interface accepts a demo alias or a pasted payment URL and shows both amounts.

It simulates a customer with USDC and 0 SOL, then updates the displayed balance and receipt.

Next: connect Phantom on Devnet, add camera scanning and require wallet approval for a sponsored token transfer.

---

Solana payment design

Proposed Devnet flow:

1. MatePay creates a payment request with a valid recipient and order reference.
2. The customer's wallet signs to authorize the token transfer.
3. MatePay signs as fee payer to cover the network fee.
4. The backend submits the transaction and checks confirmation before marking the order paid.

The customer keeps control of their tokens.

---

Prototype status

Implemented in the source:

- Merchant and customer screens, plus a synchronized demo.
- Rate fetching with fallbacks and a QR image generator.
- An in-memory alias API and a placeholder fee payer endpoint.

Still pending: wallet integration, valid QR data, sponsored transfers and confirmation through Solana RPC.

Current receipts and payment timing come from simulation.

---

First users and validation

Proposed starting segment: independent cafés and small shops serving customers who already hold USDC.

Pilot hypothesis: an ARS price and a familiar recipient name make USDC checkout easier to understand.

Planned tests measure completion, recipient recognition and confusion about fees.

A merchant subscription is a business model hypothesis. Pricing, demand and sponsorship costs remain unvalidated.
